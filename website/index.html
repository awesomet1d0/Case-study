Enjoy Nature in Luxury

Located on the California North Coast, Pacific Trails Resort offers panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean. Stay in private yurts featuring:

  • King-size bed

  • Explore the coast on your own or join guided tours
  • Start the day with a complimentary cooked-to-order breakfast
  • Relax in the lodge with complimentary appetizers and beverages
  • Savor nightly fine dining with an ocean view
